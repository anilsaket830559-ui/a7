About Windche

We watch the weather so you can enjoy it.

Windche is an independent website about windbreakers and light jackets, written for walkers, cyclists, commuters and anyone heading out on a breezy day.

  • Independent

    We sell no jackets, use no affiliate links and accept no payment for mentions.

  • Private

    Our wind scale and layer planner run in your browser and store nothing about you.

  • Checked

    Every guide is reviewed by a second editor, dated and corrected when readers spot errors.
